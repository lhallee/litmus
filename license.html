License

Litmus is free for any noncommercial use, and its code, model, data recipe, and evaluation are published so that anyone can see how a reading and its uncertainty are made. Commercial use is not licensed.

In practice

Allowed

Using, studying, changing, and sharing Litmus for any noncommercial purpose, including personal use, research, and use by schools, universities, charities, public research bodies, and government institutions.

Required when you share it

Pass along the license, its text or its address, and the Required Notice line below.

Not licensed

Commercial use, such as a company running it on its own or its customers' text, or selling a product or service built on it.

Source available, not open source

Litmus is open in the plain sense that its code, its model, and the recipe of its training data are published. It is not open source as the Open Source Initiative defines the term, because that definition does not allow a restriction on commercial use, so Litmus is described as source available and free for noncommercial use.

The full texts: the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, CC BY-NC 4.0, and the repository's LICENSE and LICENSING.md, which say which terms cover which file.
