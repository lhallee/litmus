*Short version: research software that gives an estimate with its uncertainty. It can be wrong. We recommend not using a reading alone to accuse anyone; what you do with a reading is your decision.*

2.1. The Service scores a text with Litmus, a model that estimates whether a text was written by a person, by a language model, or by both, and shows that estimate with its uncertainty (the "Reading").

2.2. A Reading is a statistical estimate from a model trained on public data. It is evidence, not proof. It can be wrong for any text, and it is less reliable for texts unlike the data the model was trained and calibrated on (for example very short texts, some languages, some formats, and text edited by a person after a model wrote it).

2.3. Our recommendations for using a Reading:

- We recommend treating a Reading as evidence, not proof.
- We recommend not using a Reading alone, or as the main reason, to accuse any person of using a language model, or to take a decision with legal or similarly significant effects on a person, such as a grade, a disciplinary action, a hiring decision or a publication decision.
