The text you check here is sent to our server, stored privately with the model's reading of it and any label you give, and used to improve Litmus. We never publish it or release it in any dataset.

**Please do not paste sensitive or personal information, yours or anyone else's.** You are responsible for what you paste. We do not store who sent a text (no account, address, cookie or device detail), but a text can itself reveal who wrote it or who it is about.

You can withdraw at any time: send the same text to the removal form, and we delete it from our storage and our private dataset within 30 days. Anyone can do this for any text. A model already trained on a text is not retrained.

Paste only text you wrote or have the right to share. A reading is an estimate, not proof that anyone used a language model. We recommend not using a reading alone to accuse anyone; what you do with a reading is your own decision.

Checking someone else's writing, or anything private? We recommend {private_page}: there the text never leaves your browser.
